# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, served by GitHub Pages from `works/happy-haus-toys/` inside the hansukun portfolio repo. It is a standalone site with its own identity; it shares nothing visual with the portfolio around it.

## Users

Parents in the Philippines who tap through from the @happyhaustoys Instagram account, almost always on a phone. They are furnishing a kid's room or play corner, comparing pieces, colors, sizes, and prices before deciding to message the shop. Gift buyers for the holidays are a secondary audience the shop explicitly courts ("Perfect for gifting this holidays!").

## Product Purpose

A catalog for Happy Haus Toys, a small shop selling on-hand kids' furniture: playhouses, slide sets, bookshelves, and toy storage. It lets a parent see every product, its color options, dimensions, what's included, and its price, then message the shop to order. Success: a parent goes from Instagram to "Message to order" with the product and color already decided.

## Positioning

On-hand stock (no long pre-order wait), sold directly by a small shop over Instagram chat. Every piece is HDPE plastic built for little kids: rounded corners, odor-free, easy to install.

## Operating Context

Ordering happens in Instagram DMs with @happyhaustoys (https://ig.me/m/happyhaustoys). Instagram links cannot pre-fill a message, so the site must hand the parent the product name and color to send. There is no cart, checkout, or online payment.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Happy Haus Toys (Instagram handle @happyhaustoys, display name "Happy Haus").
- Logo: a house with a red flag on the roof, a smiling face, "Happy Haus" in multicolor bubbly letters, "toys" on a ribbon, set on a scalloped cream badge. Only available as a raster inside the shop's photos.
- The user asked for the same playful voice as the shop's Instagram posts.
- Instagram bio voice: "Playhouse, storage, and more for your little ones 🧸 Hygienic and durable!"

## Evidence on Hand

- 28 product photos, 1536 × 1920, downloaded from the shop's 12 Instagram posts. Each has text and stickers baked in (product name banners, color tags, dimension labels, the logo badge), so they ship as tight crops.
- Some posts include dimension diagrams (Forest slide set, Shop play storage) and white-background cut-outs of color variants.
- No reviews, testimonials, customer counts, or delivery claims exist. Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Decide on a phone: product, color, size, and price must be readable without zooming or leaving the page.
2. The photo sells: the real room shots carry the product; the interface frames them.
3. One path to order: every product ends in the same "Message to order" action with its name and color ready to send.
4. Honest specs: show the dimensions and what's included exactly as the shop states them.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Price, size, and color must not depend on color alone or on text baked into images; every spec is real text.
